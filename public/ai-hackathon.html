About the Event

“We don't test what you know. We measure how you think with AI.”

AI Synergy is a case-first Human–AI problem-solving hackathon at the Gwalior AI Summit. Teams investigate a real situation, decide which problem is worth solving, collaborate critically with AI and — if shortlisted — build and adapt their solution live on campus.

Common Questions

FAQ

What is AI Synergy Hackathon 2026?

A case-first Human–AI problem-solving hackathon, part of the Gwalior AI Summit. Teams investigate a situation, decide what problem is worth solving, collaborate critically with AI and, if shortlisted, build and adapt their solution on campus.

Do we get a fixed problem statement?

No. Challenges are built around cases and themes. Teams investigate and justify their own problem selection.

Can we choose our own case in Round 1?

Only if it comes from the Round 1 case studies. An alternative case is allowed when it can be found in or deduced from the given case studies. Topics unrelated to them will not be considered.

Can we use ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or other AI tools?

Yes. Evaluation focuses on how intelligently and responsibly you use AI — not on the model brand, number of prompts or subscription price.

Is a complete application required in Round 1?

No. Round 1 focuses on investigation, problem selection, evidence, Human–AI reasoning and a solution plan. A prototype can strengthen feasibility but isn't required.

Do I need coding or AI experience?

No prior AI experience is required. Students from every discipline are welcome — curiosity and clear reasoning matter most.

Will accommodation be provided?

Shortlisted teams for the on-campus round get an overnight stay at ABV-IIITM Gwalior, subject to final accommodation rules and capacity. Details will be shared with shortlisted teams.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Eligible participants receive participation certificates, with separate recognition for finalists and winners.
